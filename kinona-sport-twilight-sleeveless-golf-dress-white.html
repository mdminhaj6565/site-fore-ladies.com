Product details

Our Twilight Golf dress is classic and simple but with a fun “flounce” in the back to add some sass. ¼ zip closure and notched collar detail. Don’t worry about feeling “exposed” as this dress is non-see through and comes with a white shortie. Worried about wearing white? This dress washes and wears easily. Simply throw it in the washer and dryer and voila! It is resistant to sunscreen.
