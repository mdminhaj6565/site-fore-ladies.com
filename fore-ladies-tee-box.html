The Fore Ladies Tee Box: A Women's Golf Subscription Box, Explained

We built the Tee Box around a simple observation: golfers rarely treat themselves. We replace a glove when it falls apart, buy a new top when the old one fades, and otherwise get on with it. A subscription box breaks that pattern, because something arrives whether or not you got around to shopping — and because someone else has done the choosing, you end up with things you would never have picked but genuinely like.

More about Fore Ladies "Tee Box"

The Fore Ladies Tee Box is a monthly women's golf subscription box of carefully selected items that celebrate you. It is also, in our experience, one of the most reliably well-received golf gifts for women, because it arrives twelve times instead of once.

What arrives in a Tee Box

Each month brings a surprise loaded with name-brand golf apparel, golf accessories, fair trade teas, protein and energy bars, and other golf-related items from small independent companies — companies like ours. Across the year you can expect a combination of name-brand golf apparel, golf-themed graphic tees, name-brand golf balls, the latest in golf gear and accessories, and custom-made health and beauty items chosen from independent, woman-owned businesses.

No two boxes are the same. That is the point of it: the pleasure of a subscription box is the opening, and a predictable box is just a slower way of shopping.

Tee Box "Light" for accessories only

Some golfers love the idea of a subscription but have no need for more apparel — the wardrobe is full, the sizing is particular, or the clothes simply are not the interesting part. The Tee Box "Light" answers that. Each month it contains the same fun accessories as the full Tee Box, without the clothing.

A Light box will include items such as golf balls, golf towels, zip bags, shoe bags, golf tees, ball markers, practice tools, and the same carefully chosen health and beauty items from independent, woman-owned businesses. If you are buying for someone whose size you do not know, Light removes the only real risk in gifting a subscription.

Choosing your subscription length

The standard Tee Box is a one-month renewing subscription that renews automatically for the period you choose. We also offer three-month and six-month subscriptions, and the choice between them is really a choice about who the box is for.

If you are subscribing for yourself, the rolling monthly option is the most flexible. If you are buying it as a gift, a defined three- or six-month run is usually the better choice — the recipient gets a clear, generous present with a known end point, and there is no awkwardness about who is paying for month seven.

Six months covers most of a playing season in a temperate climate, which makes it a strong choice for a birthday early in the year. Three months works well as a Christmas gift that carries someone through the off-season and into spring.

Buying a Tee Box as a gift

Two practical details make the difference between a gift that lands and one that goes astray. First, when you order a Tee Box subscription as a gift, enter your recipient's address as the shipping address at checkout rather than your own. Second, remember that the subscription renews automatically after the period you choose, so decide in advance whether you intend it to continue.

If you are looking for our Birdie gift box rather than the subscription, it lives on our gift box page alongside the one-time Surprise Boxes.

When boxes ship

Tee Boxes ship during the first week of every month. Boxes purchased after the fifteenth of the month will ship as part of the following month's subscription, so if you are buying for a specific date it is worth ordering early in the month rather than late. Tracking is sent as soon as it is available. Full details are on our shipping and returns page, including the Tee Box policy: boxes are final sale and are not eligible for return, though size exchanges for Tee Box apparel can be requested through the exchange form linked there.

Why a subscription rather than a one-off gift

There is a reason subscription boxes have held their popularity in golf specifically. Golf equipment and apparel are both areas where the good stuff is easy to miss — small brands do not get shelf space, and the big retailers stock what sells in volume. A curated box is a filter. Somebody who spends their working life looking at women's golf products decides what goes in, which means you see things you would not have found by searching.

The second reason is pacing. A single large gift is enjoyed once. A subscription creates a small event every month, usually at exactly the point in the season when motivation dips. Plenty of our subscribers describe the box arriving as the thing that got them back out to the range in February.

The third is discovery of the independent brands themselves. A significant share of what we include comes from small, often woman-owned companies making one thing very well. Those are the items that tend to get bought again directly once someone has tried them.

A note on value and how we choose

The items in each box are chosen for how often they will be used rather than to hit a number on a spreadsheet. That means a box may contain one substantial piece of apparel and several smaller items, or no apparel at all and a broader spread of accessories and treats — it depends on what is genuinely good that month. We would rather send something you will reach for every round than pad a box to look fuller.

The independent and woman-owned brands we include are a deliberate part of that. They are the makers who do not get shelf space at large retailers, and they are consistently the items subscribers write to us about.
