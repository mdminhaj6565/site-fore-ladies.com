Product details

The FAMARA A-line golf skort is short enough to feel cute, but long enough to feel appropriate. Made from a luxurious and recycled blend of 4 way stretch blend the skorts are easy to pull on.

The inbuilt shorts attach to the waistband to stay in place throughout the round and also feature a useful short pocket for the golf ball.

This skort is elegant, practical and sporty and comes in 3 vibrant designs that are inspired by art.
