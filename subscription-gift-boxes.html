More about Subscription & Gift Boxes

Subscriptions: the gift that keeps arriving

The Fore Ladies Tee Box is a monthly women's golf subscription box of carefully selected items. Each month brings a surprise loaded with name-brand golf apparel, golf accessories, fair trade teas, protein and energy bars, and other golf-related items from small independent companies. Across a subscription you can expect name-brand apparel, golf-themed graphic tees, name-brand golf balls, current gear and accessories, and custom-made health and beauty items from independent, woman-owned businesses.

Subscriptions come in three forms. The standard monthly option renews automatically for the period you choose and suits someone subscribing for themselves. The three-month subscription works well as a defined gift — generous, clear, with a known end point. The six-month subscription covers most of a playing season, which makes it a strong choice for a birthday early in the year.

Tee Boxes ship during the first week of every month, and boxes purchased after the fifteenth ship with the following month's subscription. There is more detail on how it all works on the Tee Box page.

Tee Box "Light" for accessories only

If the recipient has plenty of clothes, or you simply do not know her size, the Tee Box "Light" delivers the same fun accessories as the full box without the apparel — golf balls, towels, zip bags, shoe bags, tees, ball markers, practice tools, and the same health and beauty items from independent makers. It is the lowest-risk subscription to give as a gift.

One-time Surprise Gift Boxes

If you want a single, complete present rather than a recurring one, the Surprise Gift Boxes are packed and ready to give, each shipping with a card carrying your personal message.

The Fun & Games box has a combined retail value of at least $95 and contains a dozen practice balls, a coordinating hat, coordinating golf socks, a coordinating drinks tumbler, a coordinating Glove It crossbody bag and a card game for the round. Albatross steps up to a combined retail value of at least $170, and Hole-In-One to at least $220, both drawing from a wider range that can include golf balls, belts, socks, books, sunscreen, Martini tees, a hat or visor, crossbody bags, a divot repair tool, club and shoe cleaners, ball marker alligator clips, a golf rules card set, a golf towel with a zipper pocket, Orglamix self-care products, a Puma soft-sided cooler and a card game. Eagle boxes are available alongside them.

No two Surprise Boxes are identical, which is deliberate — the pleasure is in the opening. Full contents and current availability are on the gift box page.

Which one should you choose?

Pick a one-time gift box when there is a specific occasion and you want something substantial to hand over on the day. Pick a three- or six-month subscription when you want the gift to keep being noticed, or when the occasion is significant enough to warrant it. Pick Tee Box Light when you do not know her size and would rather not ask. Pick the rolling monthly subscription when you are treating yourself.

For a tennis player, the Grand Slam Box applies the same curation to tennis apparel and accessories, and pairs naturally with our tennis apparel collection.

Ordering as a gift

Two details matter. Enter the recipient's address as the shipping address at checkout rather than your own, and add your gift message in the Notes field so it can be printed on the card that travels with the box. If she has a clear preference between a hat and a visor, note that too.

Boxes and subscriptions are final sale and are not eligible for return. Size exchanges for Tee Box apparel can be requested through the form linked on our shipping and returns page, which also covers shipping timing in full.

Why curated boxes work for golfers specifically

Golf is a category where the interesting products are easy to miss. Small brands rarely get shelf space, and large retailers stock what sells in volume, so most golfers cycle through the same handful of names. A curated box is a filter run by someone who spends their working life looking at women's golf products, which is how you end up with things you would never have searched for and then use constantly.

There is also the matter of pacing. One large gift is enjoyed once. A box arriving each month creates a small event, often at exactly the point in the season when enthusiasm dips and a nudge back to the range is welcome.

What happens after the first box

A recurring question is what a subscription looks like month two onwards, and the honest answer is that variety is the whole design. Because the contents change each month and are drawn from different brands, the boxes do not repeat — a month heavy on apparel may be followed by one built around accessories and treats. Over a six-month run you end up with a genuinely broad cross-section of what is current in women's golf rather than six versions of the same idea.

That variety is also why subscriptions suit golfers at different stages. A newer player fills gaps she did not know she had. An experienced player gets introduced to brands outside her usual rotation. Neither ends up with duplicates of what she already owns, which is the failure mode of most gift shopping.
