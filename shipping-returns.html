Shipping & Returns

Apparel Returns Policy

You may return new, unworn items within 30 days of delivery for a refund (minus return shipping costs). Gift certificates are not able to be refunded.

Returns details:

  • Items returned with deodorant on them will have a $10 laundering charge deducted from the refund.
  • Returns initiated after 30 days will result in a store credit.
  • If you are requesting an exchange, please include that information with your return package.
  • To initiate a return or exchange, please use our contact formand request a return authorization.

Refund details:

You should expect to receive your refund within 2 weeks of giving your package to the return shipper. This time period includes the transit time for us to receive your return from the shipper, the time it takes us to process your return, and the time it takes your bank to process our refund request.

Refunds to your original form of payment (minus shipping costs*) are applied for new, unworn items received within 30 days of delivery.

As a small business, we would appreciate the opportunity to serve you again! Request a store credit instead of a refund and we will add $5 to your refund amount!

To initiate a return or exchange, please mail unworn items with intact tags and a copy of your order confirmation to:

Fore Ladies Golf
1800 Westbrook Ct
Plainfield, IL 60586

Your return will be processed within 14 days of receipt.

Tee Box Policy

We do not accept returns on Tee Box subscription boxes or gift boxes — all sales are final. Boxes returned to us will not be eligible for a refund. Size exchanges for Tee Box apparel can be requested by filling out this form: Tee Box Size Exchanges

Shipping

Non-Tee Box items are typically shipped within 1-2 business days of receiving your order. Most orders are shipped using USPS, with heavier orders being shipped via UPS.

Tee Boxes (subscription boxes) are shipped during the first week of every month. Boxes purchased after the 15th of the month will ship as part of the following month's subscription.

Your tracking number will be sent via system message as soon as it's available.

International Shipping

We will ship internationally, however, any customs fees incurred are the responsibility of the recipient. Please familiarize yourself with the customs guidelines in your country before placing an order.
