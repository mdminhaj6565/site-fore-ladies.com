More about Women's Golf Gift Boxes

What a Fore Ladies gift box actually is

Each box is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You choose a box, we pack it, and it arrives ready to give. No two Surprise Boxes are identical, which is part of the appeal — the recipient is opening something genuinely unexpected rather than a product she could have priced on a shelf. Every box ships with a card carrying your personal message, which you add in the Notes section at checkout.

The boxes are built around accessories rather than apparel, which is deliberate. Accessories sidestep the sizing problem entirely. A golf towel, a set of tees, a divot tool or a crossbody bag works regardless of what size she wears, and these are the things golfers use every single round without ever quite getting around to buying for themselves.

Choosing between the boxes

We build the Surprise Boxes at several levels so you can match the gift to the occasion rather than the other way round.

Fun & Games is the lightest of the group, with a combined retail value of at least $95. It contains a dozen practice balls, a coordinating hat, coordinating golf socks, a coordinating drinks tumbler, a coordinating Glove It crossbody bag, and a card game to play during a round. The coordination is the point here — the pieces are chosen to go together, so it reads as a considered set rather than a handful of items.

Albatross steps up to a combined retail value of at least $170 and broadens the range considerably. Expect a combination of golf balls, a golf belt, golf socks, a golf book, sunscreen, Martini tees, a hat or visor, a crossbody bag, a divot repair tool, club and shoe cleaners, ball marker alligator clips, a golf rules card set, a golf towel with a zipper pocket, self-care serums and sprays from Orglamix, a Puma soft-sided drinks and snacks cooler, and a card game.

Hole-In-One is the most generous of the Surprise Boxes, with a combined retail value of at least $220 drawn from the same carefully chosen range. It is the box to pick for a milestone — a significant birthday, a retirement, a first club championship, or the friend who has quietly done something worth marking.

Eagle boxes are also available, and our subscription and gift box collection shows the full current line-up alongside three-month and six-month options if you would rather the gift kept arriving.

If the recipient has a clear preference between a hat and a visor, add that note at checkout and we will pack accordingly.

Matching the box to the occasion

Different occasions call for different weights of gift, and it helps to think about it that way rather than purely by price.

For a golf-trip send-off or a league secret-santa, Fun & Games hits the right note: playful, useful, and clearly chosen for a golfer rather than pulled off a general gift shelf. The card game in particular tends to get used on the trip itself, which is a nice touch.

For a birthday, Mother's Day or a thank-you, Albatross is the natural middle. There is enough variety that something will be a genuine surprise, and the mix of on-course items and self-care products means it works whether she plays weekly or occasionally.

For Christmas, a big anniversary, or a group gift from several people, Hole-In-One carries the weight the occasion needs without you having to assemble anything yourself.

For a tennis player rather than a golfer, we also build the Grand Slam Box along the same principles.

Why boxes beat single items as gifts

There is a practical reason a curated box lands better than one nice thing. Golfers accumulate single accessories slowly and inconsistently — a glove here, a towel two years later — so the contents of most bags are a mismatched archive rather than a set. A box replaces several tired items at once and brings them into line with each other. The effect on opening day is that a bag suddenly looks and feels new without a single club changing.

There is also the discovery element. Most of us buy the same brands repeatedly because we know them. A curated box introduces small independent makers, woman-owned brands and products a shopper would not have searched for, and it is common for one of those to become the item she then buys again on her own.

Practical notes before you order

If you are shipping the gift directly to the recipient, enter her address as the shipping address at checkout rather than your own. Add your gift message in the Notes field at the same time — this is what gets printed on the card that travels with the box.

Gift boxes are final sale and are not eligible for return, so it is worth reading the full terms on our shipping and returns page before ordering. Size exchanges for apparel included in a box can be requested through the exchange form linked there.

Timing matters around holidays. If the box needs to arrive by a particular date, order with room to spare rather than at the last moment, and get in touch if you are working to a tight deadline so we can tell you honestly whether it will make it.

Still deciding?

If you know a little about the person you are buying for — how often she plays, whether she walks or rides, whether she is more likely to appreciate on-course gear or something for afterwards — we can help you choose between the boxes. Get in touch through our contact page.
