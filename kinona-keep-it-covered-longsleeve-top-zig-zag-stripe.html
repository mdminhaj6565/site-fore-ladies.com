Product details

Looking for the perfect long sleeve women's golf shirt to wear during those chilly mornings in the desert? The fabric of this golf shirt for women is lighter than that of other KINONA women's golf clothes to allow you to layer it easily under the stylish women's Layer Slayer vest. The 1/4 zip neckline of this long sleeve golf shirt can be zipped up or worn open.
