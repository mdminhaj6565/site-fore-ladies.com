2021 Guide to Golf Gifts For Women

By Fore Ladies Golf · December 2, 2021

The gift giving season is here and our 2021 Gift Guide will make it easier for you to spread the holiday (golf-related) cheer!

Gift cards are great (and we sell those too!), but a wrapped gift under the tree with something she can use on the course will show her how thoughtful you really are. Your favorite golfer doesn't have to know that we've done the work for you! Our 2021 Gift Guide includes some of our favorite things that she'll love to unwrap for herself! Shop the categories and links and if you don't see the perfect gift, contact us and we'll help you find one!

1. Daphne’s Headcovers have been making golfers smile for over 40 years and are always a show stopper at the Bag Drop. Choose their favorite animal or another option to highlight their unique style while protecting their clubs. Shop our headcovers HERE.

2. The Jazlyn sleeveless dress from Tail Activewear's Palm View collection is a flirty and flattering shade of Petunia pink that offers a warm pop of color for a fall upswing! Not only is it comfortable and flattering, it is also on clearance! Shop now before it's gone!

3. Who doesn't love a sexy and stylish sleeveless turtleneck? (We do! We do!) The Lou Lou Leopard dress, an ellabelle favorite, will have her ready to tee up in style, then swirl her way straight to a wine and cheese date on the patio.

4. Trust me, she NEEDS this bag. The Orca Saddle bag, made of a shiny, very durable, crystal carbon leather is their most popular lifestyle bag and is certainly one to make a statement wherever it's taken.

5. Give the Series 2 Pro Golf Rangefinder and you'll be giving the gift of confidence that she needs to play from the Blue Tees! She'll be able to expertly dial in distances and make club selections easily. It's simple to use and is the perfect combination of size, accuracy, and advanced technology.

6. This compact Ball Rescue Golf Ball Retriever is what everyone needs in their stocking AND their golf bag!

7. Protect her golf bag during a raining round of golf with the Sun Mountain push cart cover! It's water-resistant and secure but also a provides quick and easy access to the clubs.

8. Plain colored grips are so blah. Help her personality and putt shine with a Sweet Rollz club grip.

9. She deserves one of the most revolutionary products in golf, the Speed Cart by Sun Mountain. This 3-wheel golf push cart won the 2019 Golf Digest Editors' Choice and is sure to name you as the top 2021 gift giver!

10. Thanks to you, she'll never sit on a cold, dirty-sticky old cart seat again! With a seat cover by Golf Chic, her cart will look cute, she'll stay clean, dry, and warm in the winter, and cool in the summer! It's a bum must-have!

11. This moisture-wicking hat by Abacus Sportswear is a perfect stocking stuffer and has all of the features that will keep her warm, dry and comfortable on the course! Available in navy and light pink.

12. Whether she is a survivor, support person, or remembering someone close to her, this ball marker and hat clip set will clip easily to the bill of her hat or visor.

13. The Abacus Sportswear headband is made of a functional fabric that is stretchable and breathable. The fabric also has drycool and wicking effects that makes it a perfect gift topper or stocking stuffer! Choose from a variety of cute styles!

14. A one-of-a-kind kind of gal needs a one-of-a-kind, limited-edition graphic tee designed just Fore Ladies! This tee is the perfect way for her demonstrate her love(/hate relationship) for golf! It's comfy and suitable for everyday wear or a casual day at the range!

15. It's the perfect gift that keeps on giving... With a monthly Tee Box subscription, each month she'll receive a delightful surprise loaded with name-brand golf apparel, golf accessories, fair trade teas and coffees, protein and energy bars, and carefully curated beauty and skincare items from small independent companies. Give her a three month, six month, or annual subscription, OR just one box with the Practice Swing!

Need more ideas? We're here to help! Email our contact form and we'll help you find a gift they'll remember, love, and use.

Happy Holidays and thank you for supporting our small business!
