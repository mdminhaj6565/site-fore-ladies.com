Dressed Fore the Nines: Pretty in Pink

By Fore Ladies Golf · November 11, 2020

This outfit of the week (OOTW) is for ladies who don’t mind leaning in to their femininity… And we mean lean all the way in! This particular outfit features a super girly silhouette and even girlier shades. Yup, you guessed it: We’re talking pink, and lots of it! We are completely embracing our pink side here! If this sounds like something you can get into, check out some of the pieces we’ve selected to create this pretty in pink look!

The Top

We are going to opt for this sleeveless golf polo in a soft, bubblegum pink from the True Colors collection. There’s a lot to love about this top including the mesh detailing along the neckline and a stunning, vibrant hue. It’s a solid color, which means you can have some fun on the bottom and with your accessories without it being too loud. This is a nice, solid (no pun intended) base to start building the rest of your outfit on.

Get the look: EP Pro NY Sleeveless Zip Mandarin Collar Women's Golf Polo

The Bottom

You have some options for how you want to complete the look. You

can go for a streamlined, yet impactful vibe by selecting the matching pink golf skirt. This is a great option because it keeps the look simple but thanks to the rich, vibrant color, you’ll still be sure to stand out in a crowd with this number. It also gives you the look of a golf dress while keeping your wardrobe versatile.

Another really great option is to choose a printed skort rather than a solid color. This eclectic, rainbow print is graphic and eye-catching, featuring a super cool piping trim pocket detail! The bold bright swirl skirt is also an option that will match perfectly with our chosen top!

Get the look:
EP Pro NY 19" Tech Stretch Women's Golf Skirt W/ Ribbon

EP Pro NY 17.5" Bold Bright Swirl Women's Golf Skirt

The Shoes

Who doesn’t love a pair of shoes? To finish the look,

these Pakira Diva Dundee shoes are simple yet stylish with a pink pop of color (which we love!) They are super high quality, comfy, and trendy all at the same time.

The other pair of shoes that keeps catching our eye is these Puma Tustin metallic pink shoes — a perfect choice for those who like a little bit of pep in their step (yes, please!)

Get the look:

Pakira Women's Diva Dundee Golf Shoes - Guava

Puma Womens Golf Shoes | Tustin Slip On Womens Golf Shoes - Metallic Pink

The Accessories

Now it’s time to kick things up a notch by adding some accessories! Since the rest of the look is so bold, it’s time to go a little simple with a solid visor that will go with basically anything.For this purpose, we really like the Glove It golf visor in white. White and pink go perfectly and a simple visor also helps to tone down your look a bit.

The other accessory we opted for is one that is incredibly necessary these days is a face mask! If you are looking for one to coordinate with many of the golf outfits you own, check out this one by Tail Activewear — it's made of a light, breathable jersey!

Get the look:

Glove It Women's Golf Visor - White Clear Dot

Tail Activewear Face Mask - Rose

See you next week for another edition of Dressed for the Nines!
