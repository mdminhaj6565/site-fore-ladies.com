What to Wear Golfing in Florida in Winter

By Fore Ladies Golf team · November 17, 2026

For winter golf in Florida, pack for two seasons in one day: a light layer for a cool morning tee time and summer-weight golf clothes for the afternoon. A skort or golf shorts, a short-sleeve or long-sleeve UPF top, a vest or quarter-zip you can take off by the third hole, and a hat and sunscreen cover most rounds. If you are heading to the Panhandle or north Florida, add golf pants and a real jacket, because January mornings there can be properly cold.

Florida is a long state, and "winter in Florida" means something different in Miami than it does in Tallahassee. Here is how to plan your golf wardrobe for where you're going and when you'll tee off.

How warm is Florida in winter?

Most of Florida is mild in winter, but the north is noticeably cooler than the south. January is usually the coolest month, and the difference between a sunrise tee time and a 1 p.m. round can be 20 degrees or more.

These are normal January highs and lows from the 1991–2020 climate normals collected by the US National Centers for Environmental Information, as listed by Current Results:

AreaNormal January high / low (°F)What that means for your golf bag
Key West76 / 66Summer golf clothes; a light layer is optional
Miami76 / 61Shorts or skort, short sleeves; a vest for early starts
Fort Myers / Naples75 / 54 and 74 / 52Skort plus a quarter-zip you can peel off
Orlando / Tampa71 / 52 and 71 / 53Layers: long-sleeve top or pullover, skort or pants
Jacksonville66 / 47Pants or skort with leggings, sweater or vest
Tallahassee / Pensacola64 / 41 and 63 / 44Cold-weather layers, a wind-blocking jacket, warm hat

Averages hide the swings, so check the forecast for your tee time, not just the daily high.

Is winter a good time to golf in Florida?

Yes. Winter falls in Florida's dry season, so you're more likely to get dry, comfortable rounds than in the summer. A National Weather Service study of east central Florida's wet and dry seasons notes that the dry season usually starts in October with the first cold front, that fronts keep passing through in December, January and February, and that rain falls much less often than in summer. The wet season typically begins in late May.

For your packing list, that means two things. Rain gear is less essential than on a summer trip, though a packable wind or rain shell is still smart. And the main weather change you'll notice is wind and a temperature drop after a front, not afternoon thunderstorms.

The base outfit: summer clothes plus one layer

Start with what you'd wear on a warm day at home, then add one layer you can remove easily. That covers most central and south Florida rounds from December through February.

  • Top: a moisture-wicking short-sleeve polo or a lightweight long-sleeve golf top. Long sleeves with sun protection are a good choice if you burn easily, because they stay comfortable when the day warms up.
  • Bottom: a golf skort or golf shorts for south Florida, and a skort or ankle pant for Orlando and Tampa. Our skort vs skirt vs shorts guide helps you choose.
  • Layer: a quarter-zip pullover or a vest. A golf vest keeps your core warm and leaves your arms free to swing, and it folds small when you take it off.
  • Shoes: breathable golf shoes. Morning dew is common on early tee times, so water-resistant shoes keep your socks dry for the first few holes.

North Florida and the Panhandle: treat it like cool-weather golf

In north Florida, winter golf calls for real cold-weather layers. With normal January lows in the low 40s in Tallahassee and Pensacola, an 8 a.m. tee time can start chilly and stay cool most of the morning.

  • A thin base layer under a long-sleeve top
  • A golf pullover or sweater, plus a wind-blocking jacket on breezy days
  • Golf pants, or a skort over thermal leggings
  • A warm hat for the first holes and a cap for later
  • Hand warmers in your pocket and a spare glove

Don't skip sun protection in winter

You still need sun protection on a Florida golf course in winter. A round takes about four hours outdoors, much of it with little shade, and a cool breeze makes it easy to forget the sun until you're already pink.

Clothing does a lot of the work. According to the Skin Cancer Foundation, a fabric rated UPF 50 lets through about 1/50 of the sun's UV rays, while a plain white T-shirt is only about UPF 7, and less when it's wet. Look for a UPF rating on the label of golf tops and sleeves. You can browse UV-protective activewear for tops with built-in sun protection.

  • Apply sunscreen before you leave for the course and reapply at the turn.
  • Wear a hat with a brim. A visor shades your face but leaves the top of your head exposed. Our guide to choosing a golf visor, cap or bucket hat compares the options.

For hot spells and late-season trips, our hot-weather golf outfits guide covers breathable fabrics and UPF in more detail.

Dress codes at Florida resorts and clubs

Expect a classic golf dress code at most Florida resort and private courses: collared or golf-specific tops, golf skorts, shorts or pants, and golf shoes. Rules vary by course, so always check the course or club website before you go.

Things that can trip up visitors:

  • Some clubs set a minimum length for shorts and skorts.
  • Some courses don't allow denim, cargo pockets or athletic shorts.
  • Leggings may be fine on their own at one course and allowed only under a skort at another.
  • The clubhouse dining room can have its own, stricter rule.

Our women's golf dress code guide walks through the most common rules, and the skort length guide helps if a club has a length requirement.

A simple Florida winter golf packing list

For a long weekend of golf in central or south Florida, two or three outfits built around one or two layers is usually enough. Mix and match tops and bottoms in a few colors that go together, and wash quick-dry pieces in the sink if you need to.

  • 2–3 golf tops (at least one long-sleeve UPF top)
  • 2 golf skorts or shorts, plus 1 pair of golf pants
  • 1 vest or quarter-zip and 1 packable wind or rain shell
  • Golf shoes, no-show socks and a spare pair
  • Cap or brimmed hat, sunglasses, sunscreen
  • 2 gloves and, for north Florida, hand warmers

Frequently asked questions

Can I wear shorts golfing in Florida in January?

Usually, yes, in central and south Florida. Normal January highs are in the low to mid 70s in Orlando, Tampa and Miami, so shorts or a skort are comfortable by late morning. Bring pants or leggings for early tee times and for north Florida, where lows are in the 40s.

Do I need a jacket for winter golf in Florida?

Pack a light one. A vest or quarter-zip handles most cool mornings in central and south Florida, while a packable wind jacket helps on breezy days after a cold front. In the Panhandle and north Florida, bring a proper wind-blocking golf jacket and a warm base layer.

What is the best time of day to play golf in Florida in winter?

Late morning and early afternoon are usually the warmest and most comfortable. Early tee times can be cool and damp with dew, especially in north and central Florida. If you take an early time, dress in layers and plan to remove one or two as the sun gets higher.

Do I need sunscreen golfing in Florida in winter?

Yes. A round lasts about four hours with long stretches in the sun, and cool air makes it easy to underestimate exposure. Use sunscreen on exposed skin, reapply at the turn, and wear a brimmed hat. Golf tops with a UPF 50 rating add protection where sunscreen tends to wear off.

Florida in winter is about as friendly as golf weather gets. Pack for a cool start and a warm finish, keep the sun in mind, and check your course's dress code, and you'll spend your trip thinking about your putting instead of your outfit.
