Golf Trip Packing List for Women: Outfits, Gear and Extras

By Fore Ladies Golf team · October 25, 2026

For a golf trip, pack one golf outfit per round built from mix-and-match pieces, one pair of golf shoes plus a spare or sneakers, a light layer and a rain layer, sun protection, and one or two casual outfits for the clubhouse and dinners. Keep a full round's outfit and your shoes in your carry-on in case your luggage or golf bag is delayed.

A golf trip should be about the courses, not your suitcase. This checklist keeps packing simple, whether it is a weekend away with friends or a week at a resort.

Start with the itinerary and the dress codes

Count your rounds, then check each course's dress code before you pack. Resorts and private courses are usually stricter than your home course, and some have rules about collars, skort length or denim in the clubhouse.

Look up the expected weather for each day too. A trip to the coast in spring can mean morning wind and afternoon sun, which changes your layers more than your outfits. Our women's golf dress code guide explains the rules that vary most.

How many golf outfits to pack

Plan one outfit per round, built from pieces that mix and match. Performance fabrics are light and quick to rinse and dry overnight, so you can pack fewer items than you think.

A simple formula for a four-round trip:

  • Three bottoms in neutral colors — for example a black skort, a white skort and golf shorts or pants.
  • Four tops that go with every bottom — prints and colors are where you add personality.
  • Optional golf dress to count as one complete outfit.
  • One belt that suits everything.

Lay everything out at home and check that each top works with at least two bottoms. Browse golf tops, golf skorts and golf dresses for ideas.

Layers and weather gear

Pack one warm layer and one weather layer even for a sunny destination. Early tee times are cooler than the forecast high, and resorts often sit by the sea or in the mountains.

  • A quarter-zip or light sweater.
  • A vest or packable wind jacket.
  • A waterproof jacket, or at least a water-resistant one. See our golf rain gear guide.
  • For cool destinations, a long-sleeve base layer and warm golf pants — our cold-weather guide has more.

Shoes and socks

Bring the golf shoes you play in every week; a golf trip is not the time to break in a new pair. If you have room, pack a second pair or a spikeless pair that doubles as walking shoes, so each pair can dry if you play in wet weather.

Pack one pair of golf socks per round plus a spare, and blister plasters just in case. If you play more than one round a day, a second pair of shoes is worth the space. See our guide to choosing golf shoes.

Traveling with clubs

Use a padded travel bag or a hard case, and protect the club heads. Many golfers place a stiff rod or a "stiff arm" support in the bag so it, not the driver, takes any impact. Remove valuables, rangefinders and batteries from the golf bag and pack them in your carry-on.

Check your airline's rules and fees for golf bags before you fly; many treat a golf bag as checked luggage with specific weight limits. Some travelers prefer to rent clubs at the resort or ship their clubs ahead with a luggage service.

Clothes for off the course

Pack one or two outfits for the clubhouse and dinners, plus something comfortable for travel days. Many clubhouses ask golfers to change shoes and remove hats indoors, and resort restaurants may have a smart-casual dress code in the evening.

Golf trips are also a good time to pack a swimsuit, comfortable walking shoes, and something warm for evenings on a terrace.

Frequently asked questions

How many golf outfits should I bring on a golf trip?

One outfit per round is a good rule, built from mix-and-match pieces. For four rounds, three bottoms and four tops usually cover everything with room to swap. Performance fabrics rinse and dry overnight, so you can re-wear bottoms. Add a golf dress if you like a one-piece option.

Should I pack golf shoes in my carry-on?

If you can, yes, along with one complete golf outfit. If your checked bag or golf bag is delayed, you can still play the first round by renting clubs. Golf shoes are the hardest item to replace at short notice with the same comfort.

What should I wear to a golf resort?

On the course, follow the resort's dress code, which usually means a collared or mock-neck top, golf bottoms of a reasonable length and golf shoes. Off the course, pack smart-casual outfits for dinners and a light layer for evenings. Check the resort website for any specific rules.

Do I need rain gear for a golf trip?

It is worth packing at least a water-resistant jacket, even for sunny destinations, because forecasts change and coastal courses are often windy. A packable waterproof jacket takes little room. Some resorts sell rain gear in the pro shop, but prices are usually higher than at home.

Plan outfits around a few neutral bottoms, keep a round's worth of clothes in your carry-on, and leave room in the suitcase for the logo hat from the course you'll be telling everyone about.
