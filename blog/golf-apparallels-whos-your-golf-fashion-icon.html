Golf “Apparallels”: Who's Your Golf Fashion Icon?

By Fore Ladies Golf · October 2, 2019

Dull and drab no more, ladies golf apparel has certainly evolved into designer pieces that look fabulous on and off the course. The boring and uncomfortable women’s golf apparel from decades past has been replaced by seasonal collections with thoughtful designs, comfortable materials, and even a nod to the fabulous style of the most popular fashion icons of our favorite TV shows and movies throughout the years.

Some of the modern and stylish womens golf clothes available today are true classics — like the styles from Scratch Seventy’s golf dresses. The elegant simplicity of the Lizzie Dress is redolent of Holly Go Lightly in the ever-classic “Breakfast at Tiffany’s”. The free-spirited designs from ellabelle bring to mind Pretty Woman’s Vivian Ward, who created a look that enabled her to completely reinvent herself! Once our creative team at Fore Ladies Golf discovered these fun golf “apparallels”, there was no stopping us!

Here are some of our favorite ladies’ golf fashion brands and the iconic fashionistas that have inspired us to offer fellow golfers more.

If Modern Ladies Golf Clothing Lines Were TV and Movie Characters

Golftini: Dionne Davenport and Cher Horowitz — “Clueless”

This classic yet spunky luxury golf brand offers fashionable and functional women's golf apparel with sassy and stylish patterns. Every single effortless pairing reminds us of the socially successful pair who ruled the school in skirts with fun and unique prints.

FlirTee Golf: Carrie Bradshaw — “Sex and the City”

Much like fashion-forward and cocktail-loving Carrie Bradshaw, the popular FlirTee Golf line for women puts fashion first and lets you easily transition from the course to the clubhouse without missing a beat.

Scratch Seventy: Holly Golightly — “Breakfast at Tiffany’s”

The classically designed golf dresses by Scratch Seventy are both elegant and sporty, reminding us of the classic style of Holly Golightly in “Breakfast at Tiffany’s”. These practical designs will never go out of style and can be worn off the course as well, making them a great choice for a sophisticated lady like Holly.

Amy Sport (Spitfire Petite): Elle Woods — “Legally Blonde”

Like the spunky, petite law student Elle Woods in “Legally Blonde”, Amy Sport is the perfect mix of fashion-forward style, meticulous design, and high-performance luxury in a cute, petite, and spirited package that will have you “bending and snapping” your way to the clubhouse with ease!

StarBornSkirts: Blair Waldorf — “Gossip Girl”

The flirty and attention-getting StarBornSkirts are the shining star of the golf social scene. Perfect for the Blair Waldorfs of the world who strive to “shine in their own light”. Our favorite quote: “If you really want something you don’t stop for anyone or anything until you get it.”

ellabelle: Vivian Ward — “Pretty Woman”

Created with a rebellious nature, ellabelle ladies’ golf fashions are designed for those free spirits who like to bid farewell to boring traditional and long to stand out in a crowd. You’ll be stomping divots Vivian Ward style with their complete collection of golf and athletic wear!

EP Pro NY: Samantha Jones — “Sex and the City”

Cool, confident, and sexy, the EP Pro NY’s line of ladies golf fashions parallel the stylish and bold Samantha Jones. This exciting line offers a classic look combined with a sexy, modern flare to get the attention you deserve on or off the course.

GGBlue: Kelly Kapowski — “Saved by the Bell”

The young and spirited GG Blue ladies golf apparel line is fresh, fun, and fierce, just like Kelly Kapowski, our favorite outgoing cheerleader from the popular “Saved by the Bell” TV series. A little more flounce, a little less midriff… and all of Kelly’s iconic style.

Kinona Sport: Megan Draper — “Mad Men”

Ambitious and on top of their fashion game, Kinona's style Boogie Woogied right into our hearts. Just like Megan, Kinona has evolved because women were tired of settling and conforming, and longed to march to the beat of their own drums.

Red Belly Active: Cookie Lyon — “Empire”

Edgy, confident, unique, and stylish — Red Belly Active is the go-to ladies’ golf brand for the lifestyle of today’s busy, active woman. Dressed to the tee and ready to take on the world, powerful women like Cookie Lyon from “Empire” give inspiration to this fashion-forward, expertly crafted sportswear line that delivers on style and integrity.

Course & Club: Rachel Green — “Friends”

Cute, confident, and ever-so-popular, Course & Club is the Rachel Green of ladies’ golf fashion. In style, on point, and setting trends for every “girl next door,” this sassy yet classic golf line will definitely be the envy of all your FRIENDS.

Daily Sports: Betty Draper-Francis — “Mad Men”

Chic and sophisticated, with a style all her own, Better Draper-Francis is a woman of many faces who commanded attention anywhere she went. She stole the spotlight in “Mad Men” just like Daily Sports’ eye-catching styles that get two thumbs up from players worldwide. High quality and distinct patterns define this duo well.

Tail Activewear: Mindy Lahiri — “The Mindy Project”

Mindy Lahiri, the fun-loving, always-looking-for-love doctor from “The Mindy Project,” projects the same confident, sexy, and alluring appeal as Tail Activewear, with its fashion-forward, versatile designs, and effortless sleek styles.

Jamie Sadock: Courtney, Julie, and Marcie — “Jawbreaker”

In contrast to the dark comedy they star in, the styles worn by the characters in Jawbreaker can easily be described as bold, fun, funky, unique, and timeless. The one-of-a-kind styles and “wearable art” of Jamie Sadock golf apparel definitely stand out in a crowd, with fashion trends borrowed from the last eight decades, and details unrivaled by any other line.

Nancy Lopez Golf: Mia Wallace — “Pulp Fiction”

Offering daring styles with no fear of consequences, the Nancy Lopez Collection is fitting for women who are never content to settle. Simple styles lend themselves to iconic fashion much like that of the rambunctious Mia Wallace — always ready for the next adventure.

Define Your Golf Style and Become a Fashion Icon with Fore Ladies Golf Apparel

No matter what your fashion sense, Fore Ladies Golf has a style that fits you. Whether you are a Carrie or an Elle, a Blair or a Kelly, we’ve got the look you want in one of the many women’s golf brands at Fore Ladies Golf.
