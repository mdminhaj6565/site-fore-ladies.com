## Source facts already verified (for reuse)
- Skin Cancer Foundation: UPF 50 lets about 1/50 of UV through (blocks ~98%); the Foundation's Seal of Recommendation for fabrics needs UPF 50; stretched or wet fabric protects less; a white T-shirt is about UPF 7 (about 3 when wet). https://www.skincancer.org/skin-cancer-prevention/sun-protection/sun-protective-clothing/
- LPGA, July 2017: tour players were sent tighter dress guidelines restricting plunging necklines, leggings and short skirts; skirts, skorts and shorts had to be long enough to cover the bottom when standing or bending; reported fines started at $1,000 and doubled for repeat violations. (Bangor Daily News / AP, 17 July 2017)
- Wikipedia (Fabric softener): softener can make wicking, breathable and waterproof technical fabrics perform worse.
