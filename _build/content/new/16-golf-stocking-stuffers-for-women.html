The best golf stocking stuffers for women are small things she uses every round and quietly runs out of: ball markers, tees, a ball-mark repair tool, a fresh glove, a sunscreen stick, hand warmers and one fun extra with some personality. Most of them don't need a size, they fit in a stocking, and they all end up in her golf bag instead of a drawer.

What makes a good golf stocking stuffer?

A good golf stocking stuffer is small, useful on the course and easy to get right without asking her questions. If it fits in a bag pocket and she'll reach for it during a round, you're on the right track.

Three kinds of gifts work best. The first is consumables, things golfers lose or use up, like tees, balls, gloves and sunscreen. The second is personal touches, like a ball marker or towel in a print she loves. The third is a little bit of fun for the group, like a golf card game.

Skip anything that needs a fitting, like clubs or shoes. Those are better as a separate, bigger gift she chooses herself.

Ball markers and hat clips

A ball marker is one of the easiest golf gifts to give, because every golfer needs one on every green and they're easy to lose. A marker on a hat clip stays on her visor or cap, so it's always in reach.

The Rules of Golf (Rule 14.1) ask a player to mark the spot before lifting her ball on the putting green, and the Rules define a ball-marker as an artificial object used for that job. A ball-mark repair tool

A repair tool is a small gift that makes her a better playing partner. It fixes the dent her ball makes when it lands on the green, and it's easy to misplace, so a spare is always welcome.

Since 2019, the Rules of Golf (Rule 13.1c) let players repair more than ball marks on the putting green. Shoe damage such as spike marks can be fixed too, using a hand, a foot or a normal ball-mark repair tool. Aeration holes and natural imperfections are not damage under that rule, so those stay as they are.

Sun care that lives in her golf bag

A sunscreen stick and an SPF lip balm are practical stuffers for anyone who plays four or five hours outdoors. They're small, cheap and they get used.

The American Academy of Dermatology recommends a broad-spectrum, water-resistant sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher, reapplied every two hours and right after sweating, plus a lip balm with SPF 30 or more. The AAD's guide to stick and spray sunscreens notes that sticks are easy to use under the eyes and on the backs of the hands, which is handy at the turn. How to put together a golf stocking she'll love

Build the stocking around a simple formula: one personal item, one or two things she uses up, and one fun extra. That keeps it balanced and avoids five versions of the same thing.

For example, a marker in her favorite print, a pack of tees, a sunscreen stick and the card game cover all three. For a winter golfer, swap the sunscreen for hand warmers. Frequently asked questions

What are good cheap stocking stuffers for a woman golfer?

Ball markers, durable tees, a ball-mark repair tool, SPF lip balm and hand warmers are all small and inexpensive, and she'll use every one of them. They don't need a size, so you can't get them wrong. Add one printed accessory, like a towel or hat clip, to make it feel personal.

Is a golf glove a good stocking stuffer?

Yes, if you know her size. Gloves wear out with regular play, so a fresh one is always useful. Check the size printed inside the strap of the glove she uses now, and remember most right-handed golfers wear a glove on the left hand. If you can't find out, choose a size-free gift instead.

Are long golf tees legal?

Yes, within limits. The USGA Equipment Rules say a tee must not be longer than 4 inches, and it can't be designed to show the line of play or help the stroke. Common long tees, such as 3-1/4-inch tees made for big driver heads, are well inside that limit for regular play.

What can you use as a golf ball marker?

The Rules of Golf define a ball-marker as an artificial object used to mark the spot of a ball to be lifted. Coins, tees and purpose-made markers all work; natural objects like a leaf or a pebble don't. A marker on a hat clip is handy because it's always in reach on the green.
