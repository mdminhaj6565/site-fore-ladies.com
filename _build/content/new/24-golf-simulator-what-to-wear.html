For a golf simulator or indoor golf bay, wear clothes you can swing in freely and clean, closed-toe shoes. Most indoor golf venues have no formal dress code, so a stretchy golf top or athletic tee with joggers, leggings, golf pants or a skort is fine. The two things that do matter are your shoes and your gear: many facilities ban metal spikes, some ban all spikes, and some only allow clean, unmarked golf balls so the screen stays undamaged. Check your venue's rules before you go, and you'll spend the session hitting shots instead of borrowing sneakers.

Here's how to dress for indoor golf and what to put in your bag.

Is there a dress code at a golf simulator?

Usually not. Most public simulator venues are casual, and you can wear what you'd wear to the gym or out to dinner, as long as you can swing comfortably.

Five Iron Golf, a chain of indoor golf venues, says in its help center that it has no dress code: jeans, joggers, athletic wear and business casual are all fine, and it suggests avoiding anything too restrictive. Topgolf's FAQ says it does not have a formal golf dress code, while reminding guests that it is still a public place.

There are exceptions. A simulator at a private club, a golf academy or a corporate event may expect regular golf attire, and the host of a private event can set their own guidelines. If you're playing at a club's indoor facility, assume the club's usual golf dress code applies unless you hear otherwise.

What should you wear for indoor golf?

Wear stretchy, breathable layers that don't pull across your shoulders or ride up when you turn. Indoor bays can be warm once you've hit a bucket's worth of shots, so a light top with an optional layer works better than one heavy sweater.

  • Top: a moisture-wicking polo or a short-sleeve golf top. A light quarter-zip or vest is easy to take off once you warm up.
  • Bottom: golf pants, joggers, a skort or leggings. Anything with stretch in the hips and knees lets you finish your swing.
  • Accessories: skip the hat if the room is dim, and tie long hair back so it stays out of your eyes on the backswing.

Can you wear golf shoes to a golf simulator?

It depends on the venue. Some welcome golf shoes, some ban metal spikes, and some allow only clean athletic shoes. This is the rule most likely to catch you out, so check it before you leave home.

Should you bring your own clubs?

Bring them if you're practicing; skip them if it's a social night. Many commercial venues provide clubs, but your own set gives you numbers that actually mean something.

Five Iron Golf says you're welcome to bring your own clubs and that it also offers clubs, including women's and left-handed sets, at no charge. Topgolf also provides complimentary men's and women's clubs and lets you bring your own. Smaller facilities may not have house clubs at all, so ask when you book.

Simulators measure ball speed, launch and spin, so your own clubs tell you how far you hit your 7-iron. House clubs are fine for a fun round with friends, but expect distances that don't match your own set.

Whatever clubs you use, clean the faces and grooves before you play. Some facilities ask for it, and clean grooves give the launch monitor a truer read.

What about golf balls and tees?

Ask before you bring your own balls. Simulator screens are expensive, and many facilities set rules about which balls you can hit into them.

  • Clean balls only: the West Meadows rules require balls that are new and free of scuffs, cuts, marker ink and other blemishes that could damage the screen. Xavier's rules ask for clean balls without Sharpie marks.
  • Provided tees: Xavier's simulator allows only the tees provided, not your own wooden tees. Many bays use rubber tees set into the hitting mat.
  • Venue balls: some venues supply their own balls. If they do, use theirs.

A sleeve of new balls in your bag covers you if the venue allows outside balls.

What to put in your bag for an indoor session

Pack light: you only need a few items beyond your clubs, and space in a simulator bay is limited.

  • Your golf glove (indoor sessions mean lots of swings in a short time, so a fresh glove helps)
  • Clean spikeless shoes or sneakers, if you're not wearing them in
  • A small golf towel for wiping club faces and hands
  • A sleeve of new, unmarked balls, if outside balls are allowed
  • A water bottle (check where drinks are allowed; some rules keep food and drinks off the hitting turf)
  • A light layer, a hair tie and your phone for swing videos

Indoor golf etiquette and booking tips

Treat a simulator bay like a shared tee box with a clock. Venues book by the hour, and the time runs whether or not you're ready.

  • Arrive early. Topgolf asks guests to arrive 10 to 15 minutes before their reservation, and West Meadows asks for 5 to 10 minutes. Late arrival usually means less playing time.
  • One person in the hitting area. Stand well back while someone else swings. Xavier's rules allow only one person in the hitting area at a time.
  • Swing only on the mat. Practice swings belong on the hitting mat, not in the seating area.
  • Watch the end time. Some facilities ask you to finish your last hole quickly once your time is up, so pick a course length that fits your booking.
  • Know the group size. Each facility sets its own limit. West Meadows allows four golfers per bay, and Five Iron allows up to six per simulator.

Frequently asked questions

Can I wear jeans to a golf simulator?

At most public indoor golf venues, yes. Five Iron Golf lists jeans among acceptable outfits, and Topgolf has no formal golf dress code. Stiff denim can limit your hip turn, though, so stretch jeans, joggers or golf pants are more comfortable. A simulator at a private club may follow the club's normal golf dress code instead.

Do I need golf shoes for a golf simulator?

No. Most venues are happy with clean sneakers or other closed-toe, rubber-soled shoes, and some do not allow spiked golf shoes at all. Spikeless golf shoes are the best of both worlds: stable during your swing and acceptable at most facilities. Always check whether your venue bans metal spikes or all spikes.

Can I bring my own golf balls to an indoor simulator?

Sometimes. Many facilities only allow new, clean balls without scuffs, cuts or marker ink, because damaged or marked balls can harm the impact screen. Some venues provide balls and prefer you use theirs. Ask when you book, and bring a fresh sleeve of unmarked balls if outside balls are allowed.

What should I wear to indoor golf in winter?

Dress in a light, stretchy golf top and pants, joggers, leggings or a skort, and wear your coat on the way there. Indoor bays warm up once you start swinging, so you'll probably want short sleeves or a thin layer. Bring clean shoes if you're arriving through snow or slush.

Indoor golf is a simple way to keep your swing going when the course is frozen or soaked. Wear something comfortable, bring clean shoes and the gear your venue allows, and arrive a few minutes early.
