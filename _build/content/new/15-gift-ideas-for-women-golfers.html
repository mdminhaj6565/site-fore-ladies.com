The best gift for a woman golfer is one that fits the way she actually plays. A brand-new golfer gets more from a lesson and a few range basics than from gear she doesn't understand yet. A league regular loves accessories with personality. A walker wants anything that makes 18 holes on foot easier, and a winter golfer wants warm hands. Figure out which golfer she is first, and the shopping list almost writes itself.

Start with how she plays, not with what's on the shelf

Before you pick anything, answer three questions: how often does she play, does she walk or ride, and what weather does she play in? Those answers matter more than budget.

Then look for clues. Worn-out headcovers or a glove with holes are easy hints, and so is what she complains about: cold hands, a sore back from carrying, sunburned forearms. Match her style too, whether that's bold prints or navy and white.

If you can't tell, lean toward useful accessories that don't need a size.

For the beginner: a lesson and a few range basics

For a woman who is new to golf, a lesson with a qualified teacher is the gift that pays off longest. Good early habits make every round more fun.

The LPGA's LPGA Professionals program has more than 2,000 certified teaching members and a "Find A Teacher" search, which is a good place to start looking for a coach near her.

Pair the lesson with a few small things she will use right away: a golf glove, a sleeve of practice balls or a compact golf ball retriever that clips onto a club or the flagstick. For the sun-seeker: UPF layers and good shade

Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for a woman golfer who has everything?

Try something experience-based or curated. A golf gift box or subscription brings a mix of accessories she wouldn't choose herself, and a lesson or clinic with a certified teacher is always useful, even for experienced players. Novelty headcovers and patterned grips work too, since golfers rarely buy those for themselves.

What should I give a woman who just started golf?

Start with a lesson from a qualified teacher, then add small basics she will use at every practice session: a glove in her size, practice balls and a towel. Skip expensive clubs until she has played for a while and knows what she likes. A comfortable range outfit is a good add-on if she's unsure what to wear.

Are rangefinders allowed in golf?

Yes, in most cases. Under the Rules of Golf that took effect in 2019, distance-measuring devices are allowed by default under Rule 4.3. A course or competition committee can still prohibit them with a Local Rule, so she should check the rules for any event she enters.

Is it safe to buy golf clothes as a gift without knowing her size?

It's risky, because sizing varies by brand. If you can, check the tags on golf clothes she already owns and compare them with the brand's size chart. If you can't find out, pick a gift that doesn't need a size, such as headcovers, a towel, a visor, a bag or a gift box, and save clothing for later.
