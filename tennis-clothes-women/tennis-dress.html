Tennis Dresses

"The dress must follow the body of a woman, not the body following the shape of the dress." —Hubert de Givenchy

There's a lot to be said for the simplicity of tennis dress — or any dress, to be honest. It essentially halves the amount of time you need to spend picking out an outfit, it's always the most comfortable choice, and for those of us who love to travel, you just can't beat the packability of a one-piece garment. When choosing the perfect tennis dress, there are many things to consider: Is the cut right for the sport? Can you easily and freely move your arms? Does it allow your legs full range of motion? Is the fabric comfortable and breathable? We took all of these considerations to heart when choosing dresses from each collection. We hope you love them as much as we do!
