Our Story

Fore Ladies Golf – Women’s golf clothing doesn’t have to be a hazard!

I know what women want, because I AM a woman!

First and foremost, thank you for supporting my woman-owned business! The premise behind my company is simple—confidence is paramount on the golf course, and more often than not, that starts with our clothes. Anyone who plays knows that golf is just as much a mental game as physical, and the truth of the matter is, when we look good, we feel good. I am totally committed to providing women golfers with high-quality women’s golf clothes in the styles that make them feel confident.

I am a golfer, and as a big believer that our personal sense of style is reflective of our personalities, I found it difficult to find golf clothes in big box stores that suited me. I tried shopping online, but had trouble there as well. I knew then and there that if I was having trouble finding cute golf outfits, other women were, too. It was at that moment that Fore Ladies Golf was conceived.

I took my background in writing, web development, and marketing and created what you see here today. I truly love what I'm doing each and every day — helping female golfers look and feel their best on the golf course — and I enjoy putting a personal touch on all of my customer relationships... yours and mine included! If you're looking for something specific, please email me and I'll do my best to track it down. If an outfit you ordered doesn't seem to fit correctly, give me a call and I'll get you the correct size or work with you to get it altered. Not sure about the fit, length, weight, or color of something? I'll do my best to get you the information you need! My mission is to provide the personal service that you deserve, and golf clothing that will reflect you.

What’s your personal style?

Are you a pants or long shorts and a polo on the course kind of gal? Or, do you prefer to allow your personal style to shine through flirty skorts and bold patterns and colors? I am here for you both. From your traditional white, black, or grey ladies golf pants and short-sleeve shirts with collars all the way through mid-thigh skorts with ruffles and sleeveless tops in bright hues, I’ve got you covered!
