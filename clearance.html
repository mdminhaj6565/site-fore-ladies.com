More about Clearance

What ends up on this page, and why

Clearance stock is not lesser stock. Pieces arrive here for four ordinary reasons. Sometimes a brand discontinues a print or colour and moves on to the next season's palette, leaving perfectly good garments without a home. Sometimes we are down to two or three sizes in a style and it no longer makes sense to keep it in the main collection. Sometimes a seasonal item — a lined pullover, a lightweight summer skort — reaches the wrong end of the calendar. And sometimes we simply bought more of something than we sold.

What you will currently find spans Puma golf dresses, skirts and shorts, Kinona tops, skorts and pants, Tail Activewear golf and tennis pieces, EP Pro NY polos and skorts, GGBlue shorts and shirts, Belyn Key tops, Smith & Quinn skirts, Duca Del Cosma shoes and more. The mix changes constantly, which is the best argument for checking back.

How to shop clearance well

The golfers who do best on this page follow a few habits. The first is knowing their measurements rather than their nominal size. Golf brands size differently from one another, and clearance is exactly the situation where you cannot afford to guess — there is rarely a second chance at the same piece in a different size. Our size charts list brand-specific measurements; take five minutes with a tape measure once and you will buy more confidently forever.

The second habit is shopping for gaps rather than for bargains. A reduced price on something you will not wear is not a saving. Before browsing, make a short mental list of what your wardrobe is actually missing — a mid-layer, a pair of pants that works in cold weather, a sleeveless top for high summer — and filter by that.

The third is buying out of season deliberately. Outerwear is cheapest in spring, sleeveless tops are cheapest in autumn, and neither goes out of date in the intervening months. If you are building a wardrobe rather than filling an urgent gap, buying one season ahead is the single biggest lever you have.

Understanding "final sale"

Many clearance items are marked final sale, and that designation matters. Please read the terms on our shipping and returns page before ordering, and note the general policy: new, unworn items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund minus return shipping, with returns initiated after 30 days resulting in store credit. Items specifically marked final sale fall outside that, so choose carefully.

This is not a reason to avoid clearance — it is a reason to do the measuring first. If you are between sizes and cannot decide, or you are unsure how a particular brand runs, ask us before you order rather than after. We would far rather spend two minutes checking a garment measurement than have you end up with something that does not fit.

Fit notes worth knowing before you order

A few patterns hold across most golf brands and are useful when you cannot try something on. Skorts and skirts are usually listed by the length of the skirt portion, measured from the waist — a 16-inch skort sits noticeably shorter than an 18-inch. If you have a preferred length in something you already own, measure it and shop to that number rather than to a description.

Polos and sleeveless tops vary most in the shoulder and the armhole. If you have found a brand that fits your shoulders well, that brand will usually keep fitting you across styles, which makes brand-loyal clearance shopping lower risk than jumping between makers.

Pants and shorts are listed by inseam as well as size. Golf pants in particular are cut long by design, because they are meant to sit over a shoe rather than above it. If you are petite, check the inseam before the waist size — our petite collection exists because the standard inseam is the usual problem.

Making clearance pieces work together

The risk with discounted shopping is ending up with a wardrobe of orphans — individually appealing pieces that do not combine. The fix is to anchor on neutrals. If your bottoms are navy, black, white or a muted tone, almost any top on this page will work with them. Buy the bold prints as tops and the quiet colours as bottoms, and a handful of clearance purchases will produce far more outfits than the same number bought the other way round.

Outer layers are the other reliable clearance buy. A vest or a wind layer in a neutral colour goes over everything, is rarely tied to a season's trend, and is the piece most likely to still be in your bag in five years.

New stock arrives regularly

Because clearance is driven by what is selling and what the brands are discontinuing, this page turns over continuously rather than on a schedule. Sizes come and go within days on popular styles. If you see something in your size that fills a real gap, it is worth acting rather than waiting for a further reduction that may never come — most clearance pieces sell out before they are marked down again.

You can also keep an eye on our sales collection for reductions on current-season stock, and subscribe to our email list for member-only promotions.
