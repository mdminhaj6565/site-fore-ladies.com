Confidence starts with what you wear

The premise behind Fore Ladies Golf is simple: confidence is paramount on the golf course, and more often than not, that starts with our clothes. Anyone who plays knows golf is as much a mental game as a physical one — when we look good, we feel good.

Fore women, by women. We are golfers, and we believe personal style reflects personality. Whether you are a pants-and-polo player or prefer flirty skorts and bold prints, you will find ideas here, plus size charts, a petite fit guide and style articles.
